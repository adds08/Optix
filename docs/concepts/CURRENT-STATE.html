2 · Identity & authority: three axes, one bridge

Only the purple bridge is derived. A project tier is never inferred from a title or a login role — the dashed line is a deliberate non-relationship.

Why it matters. A person can be foreman as a job title, crew as a login role (no account), and superintendent on one job and foreman on another. Collapsing any two of these re-creates bugs this repo has already paid for (docs/assessment/07-custody-eligibility-audit.md).
